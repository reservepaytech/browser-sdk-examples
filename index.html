Reservepay Browser SDK examples

  • Pre-built: embedded form: pre-built form inside your checkout page.
  • Pre-built: popup form: pre-built form opened from your own button.
  • Hosted checkout: redirect to a Reservepay-hosted payment page.
  • Your own UI: card: your own UI with secure card field iframes.
  • Your own UI: PromptPay: your own UI that shows a PromptPay QR code.

Webhook handling lives in server/index.js and server/webhooks.js.
